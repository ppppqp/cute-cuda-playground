# Week 7 gate exam — 90 minutes, 20 points

1. (4) Formulate alternative selection for a DAG: variables, legality constraints, conversion terms, objective, and one capacity constraint.
2. (4) Solve a supplied-by-you three-node/two-option example where greedy loses. Show all arithmetic.
3. (4) Explain how dynamic shapes, cache state, concurrency, and overlap violate a simple additive latency model.
4. (4) Design provenance for a decision whose winning cost includes a measured term, estimate, and unknown.
5. (4) Evaluate your model: split methodology, metrics, baselines, sources of bias, and how you would detect drift.

Pass condition: 15/20 and question 1 must be mathematically coherent. Oral check: update the model live when the interviewer adds a memory-capacity constraint.
